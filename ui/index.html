How to read this page

The idea. You submit an ask (a request). Agents break it into features and small tickets, and each ticket moves through the steps below. Agents do everything up to “Human review”; a person reviews and merges.

Colours. grey not started or waiting   blue agents working   green done   amber waiting on a person   red something is wrong.

Attempts. When a review or test fails, the ticket is sent back and tries again. Each ticket gets 3 failed attempts per stretch of work; after the third it stops and appears under Needs a decision. “1 of 3” means one attempt has been used.

“Stuck” means one of three things:

  • needs you – it hit the attempt limit and agents will not try again on their own.
  • too slow – it has sat in one step for longer than expected.
  • blocked – it cannot start because a ticket it depends on is itself stuck.

Your two buttons (under “Needs a decision”): Accept and move on overrides the failed check and pushes the ticket forward; Send back gives it another round of attempts. Each button says exactly where the ticket will go.

Select an ask to see its features and tickets.

Needs a decision

Tickets agents gave up on. Only you can unblock these.

Stuck

Everything that is not progressing, and why.
