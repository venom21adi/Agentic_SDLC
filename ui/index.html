How to read this page

The idea. You submit an ask. Agents break it into features and small tickets. Each ticket moves through the eight steps below. Agents do every step up to “Human review”; then a person reviews and merges.

The dependency graph shows how tickets depend on each other. An arrow from A to B means B cannot start until A is merged. Dashed boxes are waiting on something; green are merged; blue are being worked on by agents; amber need a person.

Attempts. When a review or test fails, the ticket goes back and tries again. Each ticket gets 3 failed attempts; after the third, agents stop and the ticket appears in an amber “Needs your decision” box. There you can Accept and move on (override the failure) or Send back (another round of attempts). Each button says where the ticket will go.
